Common 3D Printing Questions

What materials do you use?
We print primarily in high-detail resin (SLA) and multi-color PLA/PETG (FDM). Each mastercraft piece is selected for the perfect balance of strength and detail.
Can you help with the 3D design?
Yes! We offer "Craftsman Consultation" to help turn your sketches or rough ideas into production-ready CAD models.
How long does a print take?
Depending on the complexity, most prints are ready within 48-72 hours. Custom artisan finishing may add additional time for that perfect shine.
