Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers to the most common questions about our 3D printing services.

What materials do you print with?

We primarily print using PLA and PETG on our Bambu Lab FDM printers. PLA is ideal for decorative and display items, while PETG offers superior durability for functional parts. For high-detail miniatures, we offer resin (SLA) options as well.

Can you help me design my 3D model?

Absolutely. We offer full CAD/modeling services. Whether you have a napkin sketch or a rough concept, our team can translate your vision into a production-ready 3D file. Just head over to our Custom Work page and submit a quote request.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept .STL, .3MF, and .OBJ files. If you're unsure about your file, send it over and we'll take a look—no charge for a quick review.

How long does a typical print take?

Most standard prints are completed within 48–72 hours. Complex or large-scale builds may take longer. Custom finishing (sanding, painting, assembly) adds additional time. We'll always give you a clear estimate upfront.

What is your refund policy?

Custom Work: Strictly non-refundable once the printing process has commenced. We'll confirm your approval before every print begins.

Standard Store Items: Standard return/exchange policy applies. See our Refund Policy page for full details.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale orders?

Yes! We offer volume discounts for bulk orders—perfect for event favors, tabletop gaming groups, or small-business prototyping. Contact us directly for a custom quote on orders of 10+ units.
